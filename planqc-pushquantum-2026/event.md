## Getting started

1. Check the [agenda](https://aqora.io/challenges/pushquantum-hackathon-2026/agenda).
2. Attend the Friday kick-off from 18:00 to 19:45 CET.
3. Choose a [track](https://aqora.io/challenges/pushquantum-hackathon-2026/tracks) after the challenge presentations.
4. Follow the instructions in that track.
5. Ask technical questions in the track discussion.

Use the [event discussion](https://aqora.io/challenges/pushquantum-hackathon-2026/discussions) for event questions.
